ambitious feats/ideas of our civilization

I Love when bunch of humans collectively work on something ambitious with putting lot of courage which has higher impact on civilization. Some of them still exist still in form of delusional ideas which we need to make it possible.

  • TSMC / ASML. computing at near atom level with scale.
  • Transistor and the semiconductor revolution. turing atoms into computation.
  • Apollo Mission. - we're outside of earth for first time.
  • Manhattan Project. - producing something with beyond imaginative scale.
  • Large Hadron Collider (CERN). big big big instrument to study tiny tiny tiny unit of reality.
  • Hubble & James Webb Space Telescopes. we want to look far ahead and deep in universe.
  • International Space Station. place for n number of people to remain outside of earth.
  • O'Neill Cylinders. idea to make space our next home. we need to make it happen now.
  • Dyson Sphere. idea to harness energy. big big amount of energy.
  • SpaceX (Starship & Starlink). humans on planet from 1 to 2. more to go!
  • Bell Labs & Xerox PARC. place where innovation flourished!
  • Chandrayaan & Mangalyaan. space mission with economoical cost!
