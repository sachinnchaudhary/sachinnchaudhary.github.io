Newton-Schulz

$$ \begin{aligned} x &= U\Sigma V^T \\[6pt] A &= xx^T = U\Sigma V^T V\Sigma U^T \\[6pt] A &= U\Sigma^2U^T \\[6pt] A\times A &= U\Sigma^2U^T\cdot U\Sigma^2U^T \\[6pt] A\times A &= U\Sigma^4U^T \\[6pt] B &= b\cdot A+c\cdot[A\times A] \\[6pt] B &= b\cdot U\Sigma^2U^T+c\cdot U\Sigma^4U^T \\[6pt] &= bU\Sigma^2+c\cdot\Sigma^4U^T \\[6pt] &\quad U\cdot b\cdot\Sigma^2+c\cdot\Sigma^4U^T \\[6pt] \Rightarrow\quad B &= U[b\Sigma^2+c\cdot\Sigma^4]U^T \\[6pt] B\times x &= U[b\cdot\Sigma^2+c\cdot\Sigma^4] \underbrace{U^T\times U}_{I} \Sigma V^T \\[6pt] B\cdot x &= U[b\cdot\Sigma^3+c\cdot\Sigma^5]V^T \\[6pt] x_{\mathrm{new}} &= a\cdot x+B\cdot x \\[6pt] &= a\cdot U\Sigma V^T +U\cdot[b\cdot\Sigma^3+c\cdot\Sigma^5]V^T \\[6pt] &= Ua\Sigma V^T +U[b\cdot\Sigma^3+c\cdot\Sigma^5]V^T \\[6pt] x_{\mathrm{new}} &= U[a\Sigma+b\cdot\Sigma^3+c\cdot\Sigma^5]V^T \end{aligned} $$
